# Remote work

## Schedule
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Central Time, when everyone should be reachable.

## Equipment
New remote employees receive a one-time equipment stipend of $300 for a desk, chair or monitor. Company laptops are issued by IT.

## Network
Always connect through the company VPN when you work from public Wi-Fi such as cafes, airports or hotels.
