# Paid time off

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month, which is 18 days per year. Part-time employees accrue paid time off in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1.

## Requesting time off
Submit requests in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines each request within three business days.
