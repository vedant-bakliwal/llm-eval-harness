# Information security

## Passwords
Use the company password manager for every work account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and must never be reused.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required on email, the HR portal and the VPN. Use the authenticator app rather than text messages.

## Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the Report Phish button, or forward them to security@ridgeline.example. Never enter your password on a page you reached from an email link.

## Devices
Laptops are encrypted by IT and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 1 hour.
