# Expenses and travel

## Submitting expenses
Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase. Attach an itemized receipt for any single expense over $25.

## Meals
The meal allowance for domestic travel is $60 per day, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Travel booking
Book flights and hotels through the travel desk so they follow the travel policy. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.

## Mileage
Personal car use for business trips is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Commuting to your regular office is not reimbursed.
