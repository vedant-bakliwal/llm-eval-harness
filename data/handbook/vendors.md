# Working with vendors

## Onboarding a vendor
New vendors must submit a completed W-9 form and proof of insurance before their first purchase order. Procurement checks every new vendor within 5 business days.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms for vendors are net 45 days from the invoice date. Early payment discounts need approval from finance.
